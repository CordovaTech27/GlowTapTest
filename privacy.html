1What we collect

  • Account: name, mobile number, email, date of birth, gender, profile photo (optional).
  • Google sign-in: your name and verified email from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Bookings: salons, services, specialists, dates, cancellations, ratings and reviews.
  • Payments: amount, status and a payment reference. Card and UPI details are handled by our payment partner, not stored by GlowTap.
  • Device: device model, app version, IP address and crash logs.
  • Location: approximate location, only if you allow it (see section 5).

2How we use it

  • Create and secure your account, and verify your phone and email.
  • Send your bookings to salons and keep you updated on their status.
  • Process payments and refunds.
  • Check your age (date of birth) and suggest relevant services (gender, history).
  • Provide support and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Improve the app using aggregated, non-identifying statistics.

3Who we share with

  • Salons you book with: your name, mobile number and booking details. They do not see your date of birth or email.
  • Service providers: payment gateway, SMS/email/WhatsApp delivery, cloud hosting and analytics, under contract.
  • Authorities: when required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

4Marketing messages

If you keep the marketing box ticked when you sign up, we may send you offers and updates by email, SMS and WhatsApp. You can turn this off anytime in Profile → Notifications or by replying STOP. Booking and account messages are still sent because they are needed for the service.

5Location

With your permission we use your approximate location to show nearby salons and distances. You can switch it off in your phone settings and search by area instead.

6How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is active.
  • Booking and payment records: up to 8 years, as required by tax and accounting laws.
  • After you delete your account, other data is erased or anonymised within 30 days.

7Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to staff who need it. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required by law.

8Your rights

  • See a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update your details in Edit profile.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, to marketing) at any time.
  • Delete your account from Profile → More options.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to.
  • Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

9Children

GlowTap is for users aged 13 and above. For users under 18 we need verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, and we do not send them marketing or personalised ads.

10Changes to this policy

We will notify you in the app or by email before any important change takes effect.
